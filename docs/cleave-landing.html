Splitting Yield

Lock a fixed rate on your USDG, or go long on where yield is heading. Two choices, every number in dollars.

Explore markets → How the split works
01 — THE SPLIT

One asset.
Two ways to own its yield.

USDG in a lending vault earns a rate that changes every day. We split that position at today's date: one side holds steady to maturity, the other rides the rate.

Fixed Yield Worth exactly 1 USDG at maturity, bought below 1 today. The gap is your locked return.
Long Yield Collects all the yield until maturity, then ends at zero. You win if the rate stays high.
Fixed + Long = 1 USDG earning yield UNDER THE HOOD: PT + YT
02 — PICK A SIDE

Know your number. Or bet on it.

Fixed Yield

ADVANCED: PT

Your rate is set the moment you enter. Whatever the market does after, you know what you'll have on the end date.

Good forParking USDG at a known rate
Exit earlySell any time at the market price
At maturityRedeem 1 : 1 for USDG

Long Yield

ADVANCED: YT

A small amount buys the yield on a much larger balance. If the rate averages above break-even, you profit. If not, you lose part or most of what you paid.

Good forA view that rates stay high
Along the wayClaim yield as it comes in
At maturityEnds at zero; nothing to redeem
